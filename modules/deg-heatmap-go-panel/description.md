# 差异表达热图加GO富集组合图

A composite DEG-state heatmap and GO enrichment panel driven by bundled synthetic inputs.

## Application

Present tissue annotations, differential states, module blocks, and pathway bars in one panel.

## Data boundary

Three synthetic CSV inputs: cell annotations, gene-by-cell regulation states, and GO terms. The included inputs are for plotting-layer demonstration and do not assert reproduction of an upstream analysis or scientific conclusion.

## Distribution boundary

Only the files declared in `module.yml` are distributed. Source/reference images, PDFs, internal Library records, logs, credentials, and machine-local state are excluded.
