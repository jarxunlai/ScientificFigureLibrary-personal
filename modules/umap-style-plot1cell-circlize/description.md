# 环形Circos风格UMAP

A circular UMAP presentation with cell-type and group rings around the embedding.

## Application

Combine a central embedding with categorical rings and group composition arcs.

## Data boundary

Synthetic UMAP coordinates with celltype and group columns. The included inputs are for plotting-layer demonstration and do not assert reproduction of an upstream analysis or scientific conclusion.

## Distribution boundary

Only the files declared in `module.yml` are distributed. Source/reference images, PDFs, internal Library records, logs, credentials, and machine-local state are excluded.
