# Nature同款空转生态位堆积柱

A 100% stacked-bar module for comparing spatial niche composition across samples.

## Application

Compare nine niche proportions across a sample series with a stable palette.

## Data boundary

Long-form sample-by-niche proportions with patient, ctniche, and Proportion columns. The included inputs are for plotting-layer demonstration and do not assert reproduction of an upstream analysis or scientific conclusion.

## Distribution boundary

Only the files declared in `module.yml` are distributed. Source/reference images, PDFs, internal Library records, logs, credentials, and machine-local state are excluded.
