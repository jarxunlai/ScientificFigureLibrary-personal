# Nature风格四分组PCA

A four-group PCA presentation with confidence ellipses, outlined points, and direct sample labels.

## Application

Show group separation and sample labels in a clean PCA panel.

## Data boundary

Synthetic sample-level coordinates with sample, group, PC1, and PC2 columns. The included inputs are for plotting-layer demonstration and do not assert reproduction of an upstream analysis or scientific conclusion.

## Distribution boundary

Only the files declared in `module.yml` are distributed. Source/reference images, PDFs, internal Library records, logs, credentials, and machine-local state are excluded.
